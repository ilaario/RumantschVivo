# RumantschVivo

**RumantschVivo** is an open, educational project created to make the Romansh language accessible to learners who are not native speakers.

The idea was born from a very personal problem:  
Romansh was the language spoken by my grandmother, but when I tried to learn it myself, I found almost no structured, modern, and accessible learning resources online.  
So I asked a simple question:

> If these resources don’t exist, why not create them?

---

## 🎯 Project goal

RumantschVivo aims to become a **serious learning platform for Romansh**, designed especially for:
- learners approaching Romansh as a second language
- people with family, cultural, or academic interest in the language
- speakers of Italian, English, French, and German

This project is **not** meant to turn Romansh into a curiosity or a folkloristic artifact.  
It treats it as what it is: **a living language with real structure, rules, and depth**.

---

## 🚧 Current status (important)

⚠️ **Work in progress**

The website is currently online, but:
- the lessons available right now are **placeholders**
- they were generated with AI **only to build and test the platform**
- **they will be replaced** with content created and reviewed by people who actually know the language

The priority at this stage is **structure**, not content quantity.

---

## 🧩 What the platform already does

- Structured lessons by level (A0, A1, …)
- Exercises (multiple choice and open answers)
- Checkpoints to validate progress
- Multilingual interface (Italian, English, French, German)
- Progress tracking for learners

---

## 🌱 What comes next

- Replace AI placeholders with **human-written lessons**
- Collaborate with **native speakers, linguists, teachers**
- Improve explanations and exercises quality
- Expand variants (Sursilvan first, others later)

No rush. No content dumping. Quality over speed.
